Last.fm Personal Library · v0.9.3 beta

Privacy

Updated 20 October 2026

In brief

Your display preferences and imported music metadata are processed on your device. The extension has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, account system or automatic error reporting. It does not send your XML or preferences to the developer.

Information used

  • Your Last.fm username and settings identify the profile where features may run.
  • Artist, album, album-artist and song names from your selections and XML are used to build display preferences. XML is read locally; the original file and its filename are not retained or uploaded by the extension.
  • Last.fm page content, including track names, album tags, play counts, timestamps, images, links and native edit-form fields, is read to display your library, open its normal editor and choose your personal covers.
  • Local records include display preferences, settings, the latest XML undo record, a bounded artwork cache, request pacing/pause state, and any album-less rules, discovery progress and correction-attempt records retained from earlier versions.

The extension runs on https://www.last.fm/. Personalisation is restricted to the profile you connect. Other profiles are not personalised. Redirect protection also adjusts Travis Scott and Lucki navigation on their public music pages, without saving their page contents. It does not request access to unrelated websites or read files you have not selected.

Requests and third parties

Scrolling, artwork discovery, optional read-only discovery and native edit dialogs request pages from Last.fm using your existing browser session. Last.fm receives the requested URLs, your IP address and normal session credentials. Cover images are loaded from Last.fm’s image hosts, including lastfm-img.freetls.fastly.net. These services receive normal image requests.

Real scrobble edits you submit use Last.fm’s native forms. Local display-name changes and XML import do not submit scrobble edits. Automatic album-less scrobble correction is unavailable in this beta.

No passwords are requested by the extension. Session cookies are handled by the browser; there is no cookie-reading permission. Native security fields may be used in memory as part of Last.fm’s editor and are not included in the extension’s saved preferences or diagnostics.

Last.fm and its image services operate under their own privacy policies. Browser and extension-store services may separately process installation, update or crash information under their own settings and policies.

Storage and your controls

Records are kept in chrome.storage.local in this browser installation until you change/delete them or remove the extension. The extension does not use Chrome Sync. Artwork records are bounded and may be replaced; display preferences are not evicted by the artwork cache.

You can search, edit and delete display preferences, revert the latest XML import, and export/import JSON in Settings. JSON exports are downloaded only when you request them and may contain your username, listening metadata, preferences and retained records. Keep exports private. The source XML remains wherever you keep it.

Disabling the extension retains its records. Removing it can delete its local storage. Export first if you want to keep a backup. Copies you downloaded or shared are not deleted by uninstalling.

Diagnostics and contact

Diagnostics are copied to your clipboard only when you press Copy diagnostics. They include the extension version, configured username, feature state, counts and limited error/status information. They omit individual song names, XML contents, passwords and security tokens. Review the text before sharing it voluntarily.

Use the support channel identified on the extension’s beta download or store listing for questions or feedback. There is no automatic transmission to that channel.
