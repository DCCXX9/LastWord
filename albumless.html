Keep future plays album-less

Each rule clears both album fields on matching new scrobbles after the rule was saved. Older plays are left alone. Keep a visible tab on your connected Last.fm profile; recent plays are checked every five minutes.

Export a JSON backup in Settings before using these rules. Corrections start disabled so you can review them first. They use Last.fm’s real edit form and stop if its response cannot be verified.

Review discovered tracks

Discovery reads your own scrobble forms. It saves progress after each confirmed page, checks at most 20 pages per batch, and pauses on empty responses or rate limits. It never changes Last.fm plays.

Remembered tracks

In Last.fm’s normal track editor, clear both album fields and tick “Remember blank album and album artist…” before Save Edit. A rule is saved only after the exact play is confirmed blank. You can also add a rule here.
